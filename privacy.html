1. Introduction

SimplyPet LLC ("SimplyPet", "we", "us") makes SimplyPet, an app that helps you notice and keep the meaningful moments you share with your pet. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the SimplyPet app and this website, why we collect it, who helps us handle it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

It applies to the SimplyPet mobile app and to this website. Our Terms of Service govern your use of the app; this policy describes how we handle information. We have tried to write it in plain language. Where the law requires more detail, the sections below provide it.

This policy describes what SimplyPet does now. Where it describes something that may be added later, it says so, and we will update this policy before that feature handles your information. SimplyPet is intended for people who are 18 or older (see Section 19).

2. Summary of Key Points

This summary is a convenience. The full sections below are what apply.

  • Private by default. Your pet profiles, photos, memories, reflections, onboarding answers and PawNotes are private. They are not publicly shown to other SimplyPet users.
  • You keep ownership of your photos, memories and the words you write.
  • We collect what we need to personalize PawNotes, keep your memories, run your calendar, maintain your account, and secure and fix the service.
  • AI helps write PawNotes. To personalize them we send selected information, including photos you chose, to our AI provider, Anthropic. See Section 9.
  • No AI training on your private content. SimplyPet does not use users' private photos, memories, reflections, onboarding answers or PawNotes to train SimplyPet's or third-party generative AI models unless the user separately and explicitly opts in. SimplyPet does not currently offer such an opt-in.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your private photos or writing in advertising.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, and to delete your account. See Sections 20 and 21.

4. Information You Provide

You give us information when you answer questions while getting started, choose photos, write anything in the app, or contact us. You decide what to enter. Please avoid putting sensitive information, or details about other people, in free-text answers such as custom rituals, because those answers can be used to personalize PawNotes (Section 9).

While you are answering onboarding questions, your in-progress answers and the local file locations of your chosen photos are kept on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They are uploaded to your account when you finish the questions.

5. Pet Profiles and Relationship Information

Your pet's profile and your answers about your bond make up the core of SimplyPet. We store them against your account so that PawNotes, your calendar and later features can be personal to you and your pet.

This information is private to your account. We use it to provide the features you ask for, as described in Section 8. A pet's name, photo and personality can say things about you and your life, so we treat this information as personal information.

6. Photos, Memories and Reflections

What you provide and why

You choose photos of your pet from your photo library, such as a profile photo and a favorite-moment photo. We ask for them so that PawNotes can reflect your actual pet and your actual moments, and so that your calendar can show them back to you. SimplyPet works with the photos you choose and does not scan your photo library.

How photos are processed

SimplyPet creates processed JPEG copies for upload. The app crops or resizes each photo on your device and saves a new JPEG. The current processing removes embedded GPS location, capture date and time, and camera make and model metadata, and SimplyPet does not intentionally collect precise location from image metadata. Only technical image details such as orientation, size and color space remain. The picture itself can still show people, places or other information, so please choose photos you are comfortable with.

Where photos are stored

Processed photos are stored in a private storage area managed by our backend provider, Supabase. They are not publicly accessible. We do not show your photos to other SimplyPet users.

When photos go to an AI provider

When we generate a PawNote, the processed profile photo and favorite-moment photo are sent to Anthropic so the note can reflect what is in them. This is described in Section 9. We do not use your photos for advertising.

Memories and reflections

If SimplyPet lets you add memories, daily photos, captions, notes about what a moment meant, or written reflections, we will store them in your account and may use them to personalize PawNotes, as explained in Section 9. They will be private to you, and we will update this policy before that feature is available.

You own your photos, memories and original writing. You give us only the limited permission described in our Terms of Service to store, process and display them to you and to run the features you use.

7. Information Collected Automatically

When the app connects to our backend, our authentication provider records technical information about the session, including your IP address and your device or app type (user agent). We use it to keep your session working, protect against abuse and fraud, and investigate problems. The app also records your device's time zone so your calendar and daily timing match your day.

The app stores some information on your device: your in-progress onboarding answers and photo locations, and the session that keeps your anonymous account connected. Deleting the app removes this local data. If you have not linked your account to a sign-in method that lets you recover it, deleting the app can mean we can no longer reconnect you to your content (see Section 4 of our Terms of Service).

This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics tools. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., which may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and the time of a request, to deliver and secure the site.

8. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors;

We do not sell your personal information, use your private photos or writing in advertising, or show your content to other users.

9. AI Personalization and Anthropic

The short version

SimplyPet uses AI to write your PawNotes. To personalize a PawNote we send selected information about your pet and your bond, including photos you chose, to Anthropic, an AI company that provides the AI service we use. SimplyPet does not use users' private photos, memories, reflections, onboarding answers or PawNotes to train SimplyPet's or third-party generative AI models unless the user separately and explicitly opts in. SimplyPet does not currently offer such an opt-in.

Who provides the AI

Anthropic, PBC provides the AI service we use to generate PawNotes. We access it through Anthropic's API.

What we send

For a PawNote we may send Anthropic:

  • your pet's name, species, pronouns, breed (if you gave one) and an age description;
  • the personality traits, things to avoid, and relationship details you provided, such as your style with your pet, shared favorites, rituals (including any custom text you wrote), goals, and how much time you have and what gets in the way;
  • the day of the week and a time-of-day label; and
  • the processed profile photo and favorite-moment photo.

We send this information to Anthropic so it can provide the AI service used to generate the PawNote, subject to Anthropic's applicable commercial, safety and legal practices.

We do not intentionally send your account identifier, email address, payment information, device identifiers, photo file names or storage paths, or the photo metadata that our processing removes (such as GPS location or camera details; see Section 6). Anything you type into a free-text answer is sent as you wrote it, so please do not include sensitive details.

Your permission

If you use AI-powered PawNotes, SimplyPet will ask for permission before your personal information is sent to Anthropic where that permission is required. SimplyPet will not make the first request containing your personal information to a third-party AI provider unless the required in-app permission has been obtained. If you decline, the information is not sent for that feature, and AI-dependent features, including PawNotes, may be unavailable.

Accuracy

AI-generated PawNotes can be inaccurate, miss something that matters to you, or misread a photo. They are friendly suggestions, not veterinary or professional advice. See our Terms of Service. We check each note's structure and content before showing it to you, but we cannot guarantee it is correct.

No training on your private content

SimplyPet does not use users' private photos, memories, reflections, onboarding answers or PawNotes to train SimplyPet's or third-party generative AI models unless the user separately and explicitly opts in. SimplyPet does not currently offer such an opt-in.

How Anthropic handles the data

Anthropic describes its own practices. Its current published policy says that, by default, it does not use inputs and outputs from its commercial and API products to train its models, and that standard API inputs and outputs are automatically deleted from its backend within 30 days, subject to exceptions such as safety or policy enforcement, legal requirements, or different retention arrangements. These are Anthropic's statements and practices, which may change, and they are not SimplyPet guarantees. For Anthropic's current information, see anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
